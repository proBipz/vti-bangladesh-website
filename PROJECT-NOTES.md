# VTI Website Redesign — Audit and Direction

## Phase 1: Current website audit

### Retain

- Vision Trade International name and supplied official logo.
- Company formation in 1994.
- Heritage through Modern Furnishers Ltd. and Modern Group, dating to 1948.
- International principal relationships.
- Electronic scoring, shooting accessories, institutional supply and technical support.
- Head office, corporate office, published email and telephone details.
- VTI's stated position as a DGDP Enlisted Supplier & Contractor.

### Remove

- Template filler, duplicated sections and repeated navigation.
- Placeholder testimonials and lorem ipsum.
- Unverified counters such as satisfied clients, kilometres, delivered packages and tonnage.
- Generic import/export explanations that do not describe VTI's actual value.
- Duplicate partner statements and overly broad claims.
- Old blog-style service URLs, author metadata and comment elements.

### Rewrite

- Company introduction and history in clear, factual language.
- Service descriptions around project and lifecycle responsibilities.
- Principal descriptions using each manufacturer's official published information.
- Benefits into specific capabilities: installation, commissioning, troubleshooting, maintenance, training and coordination.
- Contact copy and enquiry routes for institutional users.

### Information still to confirm before final production launch

- Whether both published telephone numbers remain active and how each should be labelled.
- Current legal/trading status wording and formal DGDP enlistment reference, if it should be displayed.
- Permission to publish principal logos and product images on the production domain.
- Named client references or logos VTI is authorised to publish.
- Real VTI project photographs and approved case studies.
- Whether a server-side form endpoint is available for direct submissions.

### Recommended architecture

1. Home
2. Company
3. Solutions
4. Principals
5. Services
6. Clients
7. Contact

## Phase 2: Homepage and visual direction

### Homepage structure

1. Focused header and project CTA.
2. Precision-engineering hero with one core message and DGDP status.
3. Short VTI positioning statement.
4. Official principal logo strip.
5. Five solution categories.
6. Local technical capability and lifecycle sequence.
7. Client sectors without unverified names or logos.
8. One final enquiry action.

### Visual direction

- Soft graphite and blue-grey foundations, warm white space and restrained VTI red accents.
- Large condensed headings and highly readable body text.
- Fine technical lines, square geometry and minimal rounded surfaces.
- Official imagery used as editorial chapters: range environments, technical close-ups, product systems and principal-specific imagery.
- Clear page rhythm instead of dense card grids.
- Purpose-built mobile navigation and single-column content adaptations.

## Visual storytelling update

- Home: a three-image journey from range infrastructure to scoring and clay systems.
- Company: the 1948–1994 heritage is followed by the published VTI leadership team.
- Solutions: product imagery supports the relationship between targets, infrastructure and information.
- Principals: every manufacturer is paired with an official representative image.
- Services: technical work becomes the visual bridge between delivery and lifecycle support.
- Clients: imagery distinguishes institutional ranges, competition systems and sports facilities without inventing named clients.
- Contact: a quiet technical image reinforces that enquiries begin with a practical requirement.

The current VTI public website does not identify individual founders by name. The website therefore presents the founding legacy in the company history and uses only the verified published titles for current leadership.
